NUMBERS FC

How to play

The first 5 players of your squad start, placed on the map in the Squad tab. You control one at a time; the rest play themselves.

WASD / arrows move · Shift sprint (uses stamina) · Space shoot (hold for power) · E pass (switches player when you don't have the ball) · Tab / Q switch · B substitutions (3 per match) · M mute. Matches last 2 minutes; coins are earned for goals and results.

Final play: when the clock hits 0:00 the match isn't over until the ball is dead – it hits a wall, a shot hits a player or keeper, or possession changes hands. Whoever has the ball can keep dribbling, passing or shooting until then, and a goal in the final play still counts.

Career mode: pick your club country in the Club tab, then play your league fixtures. Win promotion, avoid relegation, and top-division clubs qualify for the World Club Championship. Every club has a squad rating – the club you face fields exactly the squad shown in the League tab.

Your team name, club crest, league progress, squad, coins and every player's goals, assists and saves are saved on this device. Players have a temperament, mentality and personality, and teammates placed next to each other build chemistry (green/yellow/red lines on the Squad map) that boosts or lowers their stats.

Drag players to place them (the keeper stays in goal). Tap a player here, then a card, to swap. A red ring means out of position: −8 to all stats. The circle on each player is his rating.
